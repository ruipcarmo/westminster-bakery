Our story

Over 80 years of good mornings.

It all started with one small oven and a big idea: everyone deserves proper bread.

  1. 1942

    The doors open

    Our founders open a tiny shop in wartime London, baking for neighbours through rationing.

  2. 1953

    A royal celebration

    Coronation year! We bake thousands of buns for Westminster's street parties.

  3. 1978

    A new generation

    The family's second generation takes over — and rebuilds our much-loved brick oven.

  4. 2004

    Back to slow

    We bring back long-fermented sourdough, just like the old days.

  5. Today

    Still baking for you

    Still family-run. Still up before sunrise. Still your local bakery.
